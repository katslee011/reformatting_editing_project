Summary of Changes: Document

For this document, I reached out to Professor Jim Hall, who let me edit an article for his website, Technically We Write, on emTex.

List of Changes

  • Suggested structural edits for improved focus
  • Edited sentences for clarity
  • Turned passive voice into active voice
  • Fixed small spelling and grammar errors
  • Suggested a small change to alt text

Explanation of Changes

Originally, the article included more sections on how, exactly, to use emTex. I suggested removing these sections because they deviated from the focus of the article: a simple explanation on what emTex is. While the sections were well written, they were more suited for a how-to article, and by including them in the article, the reader might become confused about the article's purpose.

Other than that, the main focus of my edits was on sentence structure and clarity. I noticed some sentences had an unclear subject, either because the referent wasn't specific enough or because the sentence lacked a subject. To clarify the sentences, I suggested changes that either made the subject more obvious or added a subject to passive-voice sentences.

The rest of my edits were small corrections to spelling and grammar. The only other noteable edit was a small change to one of the image's alt text. I noticed that there were two images next to each other that had the same alt text, even though they showed two different things. I considered the context—the difference mattered—so I suggested a change to make the difference clearer.
